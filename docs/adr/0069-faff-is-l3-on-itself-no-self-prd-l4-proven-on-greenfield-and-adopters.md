# ADR 0069 — faff is L3-on-itself by policy — no self-PRD; L4 autonomy is proven on greenfield and delivered to adopters, never aimed at faff's own direction

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-14
- **Issue:** — (operator-directed architectural stance; related: FAFF-310 greenfield e2e proof, the "Immutable ends — the human PRD as the L4 leash" + "Delegated ends — methodology-derived PRDRs within the leash" projects)

## Context

faff's L4 model is a **recursive setpoint**. An **immutable human PRD** is the outer boundary (the ends, the leash); the loop may author **delegated inner ends** — methodology-derived PRDRs (per-project Definitions of Done) — and, at L4, admit them autonomously (`faff prdr admit --actor loop`, gated by the FAFF-256 YAGNI upper-gate: a loop-authored PRDR is admitted only if it *traces to a real PRD goal* **and** *does not exceed it*, adversarially challenged). The loop may rearrange the means but never redefine the ends. The PRD exists for exactly one purpose: to **bound autonomous decomposition when the human is out of the loop** — a machine-readable proxy for operator intent that the loop can check itself against and cannot escape.

On 2026-07-14 a rehome + planning session surfaced that faff's own repo has **no PRD** — `faff prd list --json` returns `[]` and `tracking.container` is unset. This was momentarily read as a gap ("the L4 leash is unpopulated for faff itself"). It is not a gap. It forced the underlying question: **does faff need its own outer PRD at all?**

The distinguishing fact about faff is that it is the **substrate** — the machine that lets other machines build autonomously. A defect in an ordinary product is contained to that product. A defect in faff **compounds**: it propagates into everything faff builds thereafter. That asymmetry changes the trust calculus for faff-on-faff specifically, in a way it does not for faff-on-anything-else.

## Decision

**faff is developed under L3 (architect-in-the-loop) on itself, by policy, and ships no self-PRD. L4 autonomous planning is never pointed at faff's own direction. The PRD is an adopter/target-repo artifact: L4 autonomy is validated on sacrificial greenfield targets and delivered to adopters to aim at *their* products — never at the meta-machine.**

- **The PRD is the leash for *absent-human* autonomy; the operator is never absent for faff.** faff's policy is architect-in-the-loop at every step of its own development. When the human is always present, a machine-readable proxy for the human's intent is redundant — the operator *is* the setpoint. Authoring a self-PRD would arm an autonomy faff deliberately never grants on itself.
- **Compounding risk is the reason, and it is specific to the substrate.** Self-directed drift in an ordinary product costs that product; in faff it costs every future build. The blast radius of a wrong autonomous direction-decision on the meta-machine is unbounded, so faff's own direction warrants human decision and oversight at every step — the opposite of the absent-human regime a PRD is built to serve.
- **The line is execution vs. direction, not autonomy vs. no-autonomy.** Autonomous *execution* of human-decided work is in-policy — spec → graft → gates → `merge-gate` → `reconcile`, human-initiated and human-supervised (as exercised repeatedly on 2026-07-14, nine tickets shipped and reconciled clean). Autonomous *decision of what faff should become* is out-of-policy. The PRD is precisely the artifact that enables the latter (it lets the loop author and admit its own inner ends). Declining a faff PRD is the mechanical expression of *the operator holds faff's direction*.
- **Two trust regimes, kept distinct.** *faff-on-faff* → **L3 forever**: no PRD, the operator is the leash, the loop executes human-decided work under supervision. *faff-on-a-target* → **L4, PRD-leashed**: proven first on a **disposable greenfield** (FAFF-310 — build something throwaway autonomously to prove the machine, where a compounding defect is contained), then delivered to **adopters**, whose PRD lives in *their* repo and leashes faff *there*.
- **Absence is a decision, not a gap.** An empty `faff prd list` for the faff container is the enforced stance. No tidy / plot / grooming pass — human or agent — may treat "faff has no PRD" as a fixable gap or author one for the faff tracking container. Silence here is dangerous precisely because of compounding: a future "helpful" pass authoring a self-PRD would quietly hand the meta-machine a self-directing leash. This ADR makes the empty state load-bearing and intentional.

## Consequences

- **No self-PRD wiring.** `faff prd new` / `faff prd link` are never run against faff's own tracking container. `/faff-tidy` and `/faff-plot` must not surface "missing PRD" as a fixable finding for faff; a future guard may assert the faff container has no PRD by construction (the inverse of the usual PRD-presence check).
- **The L4 proving ground is greenfield + adopters, never faff.** This right-sizes the "prove L4 in anger" work: FAFF-310 (greenfield cross-repo e2e) and adopter onboarding are where `faff lights-out` + autonomous PRDR admission get exercised end-to-end. The cage-acceptance and fresh-audit exit criteria (the "T5 — proven in anger" set) validate the *mechanism*, not faff-directing-faff.
- **The PRDR spine still applies to faff — at L3.** The methodology authors faff's project DoDs and the **operator ratifies by hand** (`faff prdr admit --actor human`, or the interactive accept exercised 2026-07-14 on the four rehome projects). The **loop-admit** path (`--actor loop` + the YAGNI upper-gate) is real and is proven on targets, not turned inward on faff. L3-on-faff is the *shadow* of the L4-on-targets loop, run with the operator standing in for the leash.
- **The "Immutable ends" / "Delegated ends" projects build the mechanism, scoped outward.** Those roadmap projects deliver the PRD-leash + PRDR machinery *for adopters and greenfield proof*; this ADR records that the capability is not aimed at faff's own direction. Building the leash is in-scope; wearing it on the meta-machine is not.
- **Reversible by conscious act, never by default.** This is a policy stance, not a code lock. If faff ever wants *bounded* self-improvement autonomy (e.g. a pre-approved chore queue), it requires (a) a deliberately authored, tightly-scoped self-PRD and (b) a superseding ADR — a reviewed, explicit decision, never a silent flip. Until then, the absence stands as the guardrail on the guardrail.
- **Non-goal.** This does not forbid L3 autonomous *delivery* of faff work (the everyday grafts and beep-boop drains) — those are human-decided and supervised. It forbids autonomous *direction-setting* for faff. Execution is delegated; direction is held.
