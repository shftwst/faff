# ADR 0064 — Effects-instrumentation authority split: declares from outside the actor, observes from the mechanical actor

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-12
- **Issue:** FAFF-383

## Context

FAFF-106 shipped the escaped-side-effect ledger (`faff effects declare | observe | check`): a **declare** attests an orchestrated flow's intent *before* acting, an **observe** attests what mechanically happened, and `check` computes observed-minus-declared per `(issue, step)` — any observe with no covering declare is an escape signal the Sentry kill-switch (FAFF-352) will eventually consume. But nothing populated the ledger: no pipeline step called `declare` or `observe`, so `any_escape` could never be true even on a real escaped effect, and FAFF-352's `--forbidden-side-effect` bridge would arm against a permanently-empty signal source.

FAFF-383 closes that gap at faff's one mechanically-mediated side-effect chokepoint — the `faff merge-gate` merge path (the sole sanctioned `gh pr merge` route, per FAFF-350/434's `merge-fence`). Doing so forces a decision the FAFF-106 spec left implicit: **who is allowed to write which half of the ledger, and from where** — because the escape signal is only meaningful if a declare and its paired observe cannot be produced by the same act.

## Decision

**Declares and observes must come from two different authorities, and that split is a permanent constraint on every future effects-producer chokepoint — not just this one.**

- **The declare is written by the orchestrating step, *outside* the chokepoint that performs the effect, and *before* it invokes that chokepoint.** For the merge chokepoint, that is graft Step 10 — the non-delegable integrity-floor locus that constructs the `faff merge-gate` invocation — never `merge-gate` itself, and never the swappable `ship` producer (a declare that lived there would vanish whenever the slot is swapped).
- **The observe is written only by the mechanical actor that performed or directly witnessed the effect**, at the exact point it confirms the effect happened. For the merge chokepoint, that is `merge-gate.js`'s execute path, after `gh pr merge` is confirmed to have landed (the clean-success tail, or the `classifyPostMerge` "merged with a failed post-merge step" path) — never a prose step, and never the model narrating "I observed X happened." A self-reported observation adds no integrity: it is rejected in review at every future chokepoint this pattern extends to.
- **A chokepoint that would declare-and-observe in one breath is disqualified by construction.** If the only available "witness" to an effect is the same code path that decided to perform it, the pairing is vacuous — it can never raise an escape, so it is not a valid observe source. This is why tracker-write and label-write instrumentation are out of scope for FAFF-383: those are agent-performed MCP calls with no faff-CLI mechanical witness (`faff label` emits a pure descriptor; the *agent* does the write), so the only possible "observe" would be self-report. They remain out of scope for *any* future slice until a CLI-mediated write path exists to serve as the mechanical witness.
- **Instrumentation is observability, never a gate.** No ledger read or write may change the chokepoint's verdict, exit code, or whether the effect executes — detection-only is FAFF-106's producer contract, and abort authority on an escape stays with Sentry (FAFF-352), a separate consumer. An uncovered observe warns loudly (one stderr line, naming the missing declare and the remedy) and proceeds; it never refuses. Refusing would make the ledger a merge precondition — consumer-grade authority a producer must never take, and it would brick merges on mere instrumentation gaps rather than genuine defects.

This authority split is the acceptance bar this ADR sets for every future effects-producer chokepoint named as an extension point elsewhere (label-write, tracker-write, `faff worktree-prune`'s local-housekeeping effects): a candidate chokepoint is only admissible once it has (a) an orchestrating step outside the actor that can plausibly declare intent in advance, and (b) a mechanical actor that can witness the effect without narrating its own action.

## Consequences

- **The escape signal means something.** Because the merge chokepoint's declare (graft Step 10) and observe (`merge-gate.js`) are structurally incapable of being the same write, an escape it reports is evidence of a genuinely unprovenanced merge — not an artifact of one step attesting to itself.
- **A missed declare is a visible warning today, not a silent gap.** Until FAFF-352 lands, an uncovered observe is inert (Sentry doesn't consume it yet); once it does, at L4 the append-only ledger can latch that escape into an abort at a later checkpoint. The merge-time stderr warning is the mitigation this ADR commits to: the omission is surfaced at the moment it happens, not discovered later as a latched abort. If calibration ever shows recurring false escapes from legitimate orchestrated merges, the sanctioned fix is making the graft-side declare more mechanical (e.g. generated rather than hand-maintained prose) — never relaxing the observe, and never having the chokepoint self-declare.
- **Every future effects-producer chokepoint inherits this bar, not just merge.** A design that proposes pairing a declare and observe from the same call site, or that has an agent self-report an observe in prose, is rejected under this ADR without needing a fresh design conversation each time. The two extension points this FAFF-383 slice explicitly named out of scope (tracker/label-write; local housekeeping in `faff worktree-prune`) are exactly the two currently-known cases where no qualifying mechanical witness yet exists — they stay out of scope until one does.
- **The shared `appendEffectEntries` helper is the single ledger writer, but it is authority-blind by design.** It validates and appends descriptors for whichever caller invokes it (`cmdEffects` for `declare`/`observe`, `merge-gate.js` for its mechanical observe); the authority split above is enforced by *which call sites exist and where they live in the codebase*, not by anything the helper itself checks. A future chokepoint that violates the split (e.g. `merge-gate.js` calling `appendEffectEntries` with `kind_of_entry: "declare"`) would not be caught mechanically — it is caught in review, per this ADR.
- **If a future slice needs a CLI-mediated tracker or label write path** (discharging the two named extension points), that path becomes the mechanical witness those chokepoints were missing, and the same declare-outside/observe-inside split applies to it unchanged — this ADR does not need to be revisited to admit it, only satisfied.
