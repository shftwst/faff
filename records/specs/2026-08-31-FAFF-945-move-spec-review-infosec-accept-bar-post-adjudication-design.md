# Move the spec-review infosec accept-bar from pre-judge residue to post-adjudication

> Spec: faffter-dark-nlspec · 2026-08-31 · autonomous · claude-code/unknown · confidence: high. Full spec on Linear FAFF-945.

## Why

The spec-review judge (FAFF-922) rules `accept | keep-going | park-needs-human` at the would-be-park point. A deterministic accept-bar then coerces a judge `accept` to `park-needs-human` whenever `evidence.infosec_major_free_latest == false`. That signal is derived in `spec-judge-evidence.js` from the **latest round's refuter objections** — the objection set *before* the judge rules. So an `accept` (the judge has just down-weighted every standing infosec objection to not-real or taste) is still forced to a human park because a raw refuter infosec major was ever raised.

Observed live on FAFF-930 round 5: the judge accepted, but the pre-judge floor re-escalated on infosec majors the judge had already resolved. On the nightly L3 drain this false-escalates on essentially every security-adjacent spec, including the obvious and fixable ones. This is the single biggest lever on "overnight runs leave a pile of parks".

The floor's real job is narrow: stop a compromised `accept`-and-ship that down-weights a genuine security hole to taste. Moving it from the pre-judge residue to the post-adjudication ledger keeps that guard where it belongs (an infosec major the judge leaves *standing* still parks) while dropping the false parks (an infosec major the judge *resolved* no longer parks).

## What

Move the infosec floor so it reads the **post-adjudication ledger** (the judge's own ruling), not the pre-judge refuter residue.

**Chosen:** Add a deterministic accept-bar roll-up — `faff spec-judge-accept-bar` — that takes the evidence bundle, the judge's conformant `spec-judge-verdict`, and the level, and returns the coerced disposition. It replaces the hand-arithmetic the accept-bar currently does in faff-prep prose with a single tested seam ("arithmetic over the bundle, not the judge's read"), and encodes the level-aware infosec floor. Rationale: the coercion is pure arithmetic over two JSON inputs; a tested CLI is the faff-idiomatic home (mirrors `spec-judge-evidence`, `spec-review-convergence`) and removes the level branch from fragile prose.

**Chosen:** The infosec floor is level-aware. **The accept-bar only ever runs at L3–L4.** The spec-review judge (FAFF-922) whose verdict the accept-bar coerces is off by default when a human is present and runs at the autonomous levels **L3–L4 only** (faff-prep → _Spec-review judge_); L1 and L2 are supervised/interactive and never reach the judge or the accept-bar, so there is no L1/L2 accept-bar path to fail open. The authority gate's `accept-provisional` "L3 (and below)" wording is the arithmetic default for a non-L4 invocation; in practice the accept-bar is invoked only at **L3** (which carries the audit-trail-and-dissent morning backstop below) and **L4** (which keeps the interim pre-judge floor). This is why mitigation (2) is scoped to L3 — L3 is the only human-absent level the accept-bar's non-coercing accept path is reachable at, and L4 keeps the pre-judge floor instead.
- **L1–L3:** post-adjudication. The floor fires only if the judge's post-ruling standing set (`verdict.upheld[]`) still carries an infosec objection at `major` or `blocker`. A conformant `accept` upholds none by contract (`contract-defs.js` enforces `accept` ⇒ empty `upheld`), so an `accept` never fires the infosec floor at L3. An unresolved infosec major means the judge returned `park-needs-human` (upholding it), which parks through the verdict itself — the accept-bar never sees an accept to coerce. Net effect: L3 stops parking on infosec objections the judge accepted.
  - **The honest description of the L1–L3 change is: the pre-judge infosec floor is *removed*, and the post-adjudication scan is intentionally non-firing under today's contract.** That removal IS the fix — it is what stops the false parks. Under the current three-verdict contract a conformant `accept` upholds none, so `infosecMajorFree(verdict.upheld)` is always `true` for an `accept` and the L1–L3 infosec floor never coerces. The security guarantee at L1–L3 becomes defense-in-depth rather than a pre-emptive gate: (1) the judge itself parks any infosec major it leaves standing (a non-`accept` verdict never reaches the accept-bar's accept path); (2) the L3 promote-**provisional** authority-gate row logs the evidence bundle + rationale, and a dissenting morning human comment re-parks/reverts (the audit-trail-and-dissent backstop already used for L3-provisional accepts); (3) the implementing code is separately reviewed at graft (code-review + holdout). The residual L1–L3 exposure — a judge that mis-partitions a real infosec major into `downweighted[]` and returns `accept` — is the explicit judge-trust trade this ticket buys at L1–L3, bounded by (2) and (3).
  - **The `upheld`-scan is written now as the forward seam, but the accept-bar does not police the contract.** The scan is a genuine no-op under today's contract, kept because it is exactly the predicate the punted four-outcome vocabulary will make live: once the `spec-judge-verdict` contract is widened so a conformant `accept` may carry an auto-applied+verified `UPHOLD_REVIEW` correction (an infosec proposition resolved *by fixing it*, still recorded on `upheld[]`), the scan coerces on any infosec major that is *still* standing. The accept-bar reaches that future without a rewrite precisely because it **does not** reject an `accept`-with-`upheld` as invalid — enforcing the `accept ⇒ empty upheld` invariant is the contract layer's job (`faff contract spec-judge-verdict`), not the accept-bar's. See the next Chosen.
- **L4:** the current **pre-judge** infosec floor is retained as an interim backstop (`evidence.infosec_major_free_latest == false` still coerces an `accept` to `park-needs-human`). L4 has no human backstop, and full judge-trust at L4 is only sound with a durable, interrogatable audit trail that does not exist yet. L4 is flipped to full-trust (no infosec park) by the sibling below, not here.

**Chosen:** The accept-bar consumes an already-validated verdict; it does **not** re-police the `accept ⇒ empty upheld` invariant. faff-prep validates the judge's `faff-contract:spec-judge-verdict` block through `faff contract spec-judge-verdict` (exit 0) **before** calling the accept-bar; a non-conformant verdict parks upstream and never reaches `computeAcceptBar`. So `computeAcceptBar` treats its `verdict` input as conformant-by-precondition and simply applies blocker floor + level-aware infosec scan + authority gate over it. This is what avoids the forward-incompatibility trap: today `accept ⇒ empty upheld` (the scan is a no-op); when the four-outcome contract widens to allow `accept` + non-empty `upheld[]`, the same code coerces on a still-standing infosec major with no change. The accept-bar never exit-2s on an `accept`-with-`upheld` — that shape is the contract layer's concern.

**Chosen:** Fail-closed only on genuine plumbing faults, at every level. The accept-bar is the sole enforcement point and at L4 there is no human backstop, so it must never fail *open* on an I/O or parse failure. Exit 2 (no `disposition` on stdout) on: a non-object evidence bundle or verdict; an unreadable `--evidence`/`--verdict` file; a body that does not `JSON.parse`; or a `verdict.verdict` outside the closed three (`accept`/`keep-going`/`park-needs-human`). A merely-missing `blocker_free_latest`/`infosec_major_free_latest` field is the fail-safe (the floor fires — coerce to `park-needs-human`), never clear. faff-prep (step 5) treats **any** non-zero accept-bar exit, or an absent/empty `disposition`, as `park-needs-human` — a read/parse failure parks, it never ships.

**Chosen:** The `upheld[]` / `downweighted[]` objection entry shape is `{ lens, severity }` per the `spec-judge-verdict` contract in `contract-defs.js` (`lens` ∈ {architectural, infosec, methodology, QA}; `severity` ∈ {blocker, major, minor}). The L1–L3 infosec scan reuses the existing `infosecMajorFree` predicate — `false` iff any entry has `lens === "infosec"` and `severity ∈ {major, blocker}` — over `verdict.upheld` rather than the pre-judge `standing_objections`. Test fixtures use this shape.

**Chosen:** The blocker floor is unchanged. `evidence.blocker_free_latest == false` still coerces an `accept` to `park-needs-human` at every level. This ticket is scoped to the infosec floor only.

**Chosen:** The evidence bundle is unchanged. `infosec_major_free_latest` stays in the bundle as advisory judge input (the judge should weigh that an infosec major was raised); it simply stops being the L1–L3 coercion source. Only the L4 interim floor still reads it.

**Chosen:** Split the durable audit trail + L4 full-trust into a sibling ticket that FAFF-945 blocks. The operator decision couples L4 no-infosec-park to a durable, interrogatable trail of (objections + judgements + built specs) with a query path. Today that data is scattered and ephemeral (run-dir `round-<n>.json` / judge verdicts, partial PR anchor, `.faff/calibration/`); making it a coherent queryable surface (extend `faff events` / `faff audit` or a reader) is a separate build with its own persistence-schema decision. It is too large to fold safely into this arithmetic move, and L4-no-park must not ship before it. This ticket ships the accept-bar move and the L3 path (safe via the existing provisional trail); the sibling ships the durable trail and flips L4.

**Punt:** The richer four-outcome per-proposition judge vocabulary (`UPHOLD_REVIEW` with an auto-applied + verified correction, `AFFIRM_SPEC`, `PRD_BOUNDARY`) is not in the current `spec-judge-verdict` contract (`accept | keep-going | park-needs-human` with `upheld[]`/`downweighted[]`). At the current granularity, a judge that finds an infosec major real-but-fixable returns a non-`accept`; the auto-apply-and-verify path is future work. This slice moves the floor within the existing contract; enriching the judge vocabulary is out of scope (relates to FAFF-935).

**Assumes:** The `--evidence` and `--verdict` inputs are local, operator-supplied files (the same trust model as the existing `spec-judge-evidence.js` round-record reads) — not network input — so no input size or parse-depth bound is imposed beyond the fail-closed JSON-parse check.

**Assumes:** The accept-bar only ever coerces an `accept` verdict — `keep-going` and `park-needs-human` route through the authority gate unchanged (matching today's faff-prep prose). The authority gate (coerced verdict × level → promote-final / promote-provisional / park) is unchanged.

## How

1. **`spec-judge-evidence.js`** — add a pure `computeAcceptBar(evidence, verdict, level)` co-located with `infosecMajorFree` (this file is the accept-bar / admit roll-up home). It:
   - **pass-through** for `keep-going` / `park-needs-human` (the accept-bar only coerces `accept`): return `{ disposition: <the verdict verbatim>, coerced_from: null, floor_fired: null, level }`;
   - for `accept`: coerce to `park-needs-human` if `evidence.blocker_free_latest !== true` (blocker floor, `floor_fired: "blocker"`); else apply the infosec floor — at `L4`, coerce if `evidence.infosec_major_free_latest === false`; at `L1`–`L3`, coerce only if `verdict.upheld` carries an `infosec` objection at `major`/`blocker` (reusing the `infosecMajorFree` predicate over `upheld`) — a fired infosec floor sets `floor_fired: "infosec"`, `disposition: "park-needs-human"`, `coerced_from: "accept"`;
   - an **uncoerced** `accept` runs the authority gate: `disposition: "accept-final"` at L4, `"accept-provisional"` at L3 (and below), with `coerced_from: null`, `floor_fired: null`;
   - returns the fixed shape `{ disposition, coerced_from, floor_fired, level }` as JSON on stdout (`disposition` ∈ {`accept-final`, `accept-provisional`, `park-needs-human`, `keep-going`}; `coerced_from` is `"accept"` only on a coerced accept, else `null`; `floor_fired` is `"blocker"|"infosec"` only when a floor fired, else `null`).
   - **Fail-closed (exit 2, no disposition on stdout)** on genuine plumbing faults only: a non-object evidence bundle or verdict; an unreadable `--evidence`/`--verdict` file; a body that does not `JSON.parse`; a `verdict.verdict` outside the closed three. It does **not** exit-2 on an `accept`-with-`upheld` — that invariant is the contract layer's (`faff contract spec-judge-verdict`, run by faff-prep before this CLI). A missing `blocker_free_latest`/`infosec_major_free_latest` field is the fail-safe (floor fires — coerce), not a fault.
2. **`bin/faff`** — register `spec-judge-accept-bar` → `cmdSpecJudgeAcceptBar` (flags: `--evidence <file|->`, `--verdict <file|->`, `--level <L1..L4>`), mirroring the `spec-judge-evidence` registration.
3. **`lint-cli-coverage.js`** — add the `spec-judge-accept-bar` → test-file entry so `faff lint-cli-coverage` stays green.
4. **`test/spec-judge-accept-bar.test.mjs`** — new test file. Every fixture uses the `{lens, severity}` entry shape and a fully-enumerated evidence bundle (`blocker_free_latest`, `infosec_major_free_latest` both set). `computeAcceptBar` is documented to assume upstream contract validation, so a fixture may carry an `accept` with a non-empty `upheld[]` to exercise the future-contract firing path directly at the unit boundary. Cases:
   - **accept + clean** (`blocker_free_latest:true`, `infosec_major_free_latest:true`, `upheld:[]`) → `accept-provisional` at L1, L2, L3 (all three asserted, closing the L1/L2 gap); `accept-final` at L4. All assert `coerced_from:null`, `floor_fired:null`.
   - **accept + pre-judge infosec major, empty `upheld`** (`infosec_major_free_latest:false`, `upheld:[]`) → **not** coerced at L3 (`accept-provisional`, `coerced_from:null`) — the regression this ticket fixes — and **coerced** at L4 (`park-needs-human`, `coerced_from:"accept"`, `floor_fired:"infosec"`) — the interim floor.
   - **accept + `upheld` carries an infosec major, `infosec_major_free_latest:true`** (`upheld:[{lens:"infosec",severity:"major"}]`, representing the widened future contract, with no pre-judge major so the L4 floor is not what is under test) → at **L1/L2/L3** the post-adjudication scan **fires**: `park-needs-human`, `coerced_from:"accept"`, `floor_fired:"infosec"` (the observable firing path for the scan). At **L4** the `upheld`-scan does **not** run (L4's floor is the pre-judge `infosec_major_free_latest`, which is `true` here) → `accept-final`. This case is what pins that the scan is L1–L3-only and L4 is pre-judge-only.
   - **accept + standing blocker** (`blocker_free_latest:false`) → `park-needs-human`, `coerced_from:"accept"`, `floor_fired:"blocker"`, at every level.
   - **`keep-going` / `park-needs-human`** → pass through unchanged: `disposition` = the verbatim verdict, `coerced_from:null`, `floor_fired:null`.
   - **fail-closed** → exit 2 on: non-object evidence/verdict, unreadable file, non-JSON body, `verdict.verdict` outside the closed three. (No exit-2 on `accept`-with-`upheld` — that is the contract layer's, not the accept-bar's.)
5. **`faff-prep/SKILL.md`** — rewire the "Deterministic accept-bar" section to shell `faff spec-judge-accept-bar --evidence <bundle> --verdict <judge-verdict> --level <level>` and route on its `disposition` (`accept-final`/`accept-provisional` → the authority-gate promote rows; `park-needs-human` → park). State that **any** non-zero accept-bar exit or an absent/empty `disposition` routes to `park-needs-human` (fail-closed — a read/parse failure parks, never ships). Update the accept-bar description to the post-adjudication (L1–L3, a defensive belt with defense-in-depth) vs interim pre-judge (L4) infosec floor and the sibling dependency. Keep the authority-gate table.
