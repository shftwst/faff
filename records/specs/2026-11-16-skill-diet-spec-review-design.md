# Default spec-review producer diet

## Scope and proof

Shorten repeated role, output and level-gradient explanation. Preserve the whole Inputs, lens checklist, objection restraint/enrichment, heading-slug rule, methodology consumption and verdict roll-up byte-for-byte. Keep loader headings and all mandatory reads. The cut saves 1,405 bytes and about 344 tokens.

Six actual verdicts per sample use the existing three spec-verdict fixtures, two code-blind QA defects and a methodology blocker. Require five Sol-medium samples per arm, native audit, manual review, actual contract/routing consumption and founded-verdict negative controls. Fifteen policy questions are separate. No prep-loop, hosted tracker or human-escalation execution claim.
