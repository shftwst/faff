# Beep-boop reporting skill diet

## Scope and proof

Shorten repeated Human follow-ups and outcome bucket explanations in Reporting. Command and facts schema, rendering pass, run close, peer handling, continuation guard, tracker/conversation publication, Stopping condition and all other sections remain byte-identical. No new mandatory reads.

The paired boundary executes the pinned production summary handler against real disposable ledgers. External dependencies, rendering, publication and continuation are controlled. Six execution cases plus independent reading cover actual facts, summaries and publications. This is not a full build loop or hosted publication test. Unchanged peer and pre-write normalisation behaviour is outside scope. Static review retains routed-out and budget variants beyond the model fixture. Evidence: verification/experiments/2026-11-16-skill-diet/.
