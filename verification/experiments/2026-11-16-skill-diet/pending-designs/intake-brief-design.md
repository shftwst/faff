# intake-brief skill diet

## Scope and proof

Frontmatter, Input, the whole discovery procedure, Markdown template and Appetite remain byte-identical. Six completed conversation snapshots per sample measure final-brief facts, mode/sections, constraints, dependencies, non-goals and open questions. Twelve policy decisions are separate. No elicitation or downstream ticket shaping is claimed.

Require five fresh Astra-low samples per arm, complete native audits, manual review of every artifact and integration checks. Evidence: verification/experiments/2026-11-16-skill-diet/. No new mandatory reads.
