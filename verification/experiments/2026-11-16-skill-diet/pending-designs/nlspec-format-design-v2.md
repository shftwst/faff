# Full nlspec format skill diet

## Scope and proof

Shorten preamble, WHY through basic HOW mechanics, decision rationale and DONE explanations/examples. Failure modes, anti-pattern/rule-inventory, temporal anchors, Scenarios, Open Questions/Assumptions, Confidence, inherited self-review, contract artifact and Rules remain byte-identical. No new mandatory reads.

Four final-spec cases cover mechanical changes, retry/privacy and holdout separation, blocking decisions and eligible deferred decisions. All actual artifacts require independent requirement-to-DONE, source-fact and marker review. Real pinned contract, admissibility, criterion classification, punt scanning and prep attachment run in disposable projects. Tracker publication, inherited self-review and the full prep lifecycle are outside this measurement.

Evidence: verification/experiments/2026-11-16-skill-diet/.
