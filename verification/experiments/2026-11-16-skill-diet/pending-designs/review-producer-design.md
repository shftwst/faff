# Default-review skill diet

## Scope and proof

Consolidate pre-PR rationale, passes 1-4, repeated output-contract prose and
appetite rationale. Preserve the complete human-judgement pass and appetite
table byte-for-byte. Keep the accepted discovery description unchanged. No new
mandatory reads and no change to the fixed CLI contract.

Seven controlled caller snapshots produce actual persisted reviews consumed by
the real review-verdict CLI; twenty decisions are reported separately. Require
five fresh V2 baseline and candidate samples, all intended cases, complete native
audits and manual reading of all seventy artifacts. V1's unchanged producer was
correctly refusing missing coverage but its assertion wording missed the oracle's
literal words; preserve that failed report and use the preregistered V2 correction.
No full graft execution, real test/build execution or irreversible-action claim.

Evidence lives under verification/experiments/2026-11-16-skill-diet/.
The review-producer-v2 plans bind the fixture, candidate and fixed runtime.
