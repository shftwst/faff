## Scope and proof

six-verdict conditions, standalone reference reads, display section, frontmatter
and eval section anchors remain byte-identical. No new mandatory read is added.

Require five fresh Astra-low samples per arm: ten assignments with the real Punt
scanner and routing contract, two validation refusals and thirteen separate
decisions. Compare every extraction and consumer result, including root cause;
require complete native audits. Display and full queue/park orchestration are
outside this slice. Evidence is in verification/experiments/2026-11-16-skill-diet/.
