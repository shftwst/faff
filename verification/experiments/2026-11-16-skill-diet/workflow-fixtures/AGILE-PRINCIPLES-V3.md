# Agile diagnosis V3 review boundary

V1 and V2 rejected source-faithful recommendations because they used different
verbs: moving, propose a home, and declare a blocker. The original failed reports
and artifacts remain preserved. Expanding a verb whitelist is not a reliable
semantic judge.

V3 keeps automated checks for the banner, named source identifiers, principle and
nonempty diagnosis/action. Empty-output cases and eighteen decisions retain their
exact checks. Every actual artifact must separately pass the frozen manual rubric
in the fixture: factual situation, consequence, correct actionable recommendation,
no invented facts, no unsupported extra finding and no change in write authority.
The required manual review cannot be replaced by a mechanical pass. Both arms have
five fresh Astra-low samples; all eighty artifacts are read and hash-bound before
source acceptance. No older sample is pooled and no complete caller proof is claimed.
