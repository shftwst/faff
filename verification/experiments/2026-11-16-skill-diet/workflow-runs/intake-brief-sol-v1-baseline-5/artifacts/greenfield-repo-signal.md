# Discovery brief: Local rehearsal recordings

mode: greenfield

## Goal
Let band members organise local rehearsal recordings.

## Capabilities
- Recording catalogue: organise local rehearsal recordings.
- Search by rehearsal date: find recordings by their rehearsal date.

## First slice
List manually selected local recordings by rehearsal date.

## Constraints
- TypeScript and a browser UI, specified in the README and confirmed by the human.
- Files remain local.

## Out of scope
- Cloud upload.
- Audio editing.

## Open questions
- How should recordings without a date be shown? Carry forward for faff-prep.
